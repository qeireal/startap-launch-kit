# Free startup directories

Generated from https://startap.cc/tools/startup-directories. Last checked October 2026. Terms change often, so open each submit page before relying on a row.

Columns:
- **DR**: Ahrefs Domain Rating, only where it was checked independently, with the date. "–" means unknown, not low.
- **Account**: the free path needs an account or sign-in.
- **Badge**: "Required" means the free path needs their badge or a backlink on your site. "Optional" means a badge buys a followed link or a faster review.
- **Wait**: how long until the listing goes live on the free path.
